One rule above all: one thing is loud

HIERARCHY.md ranks every visual on six loudness tiers, and each rule now has research and a number behind it. The checks run on every change.

1

Moments and rewards

Owns the screen for a beat, one at a time, skippable. The world dims behind it.

2

Combat and impact

Bright on the onset frame and back to baseline within twelve frames. Threats outrank loot.

3

HUD and interface

Still until a number changes, then it says exactly what changed.

4

World and atmosphere

A muted value range and slow ambient motion. It never competes with play.

5

Materials

Rarity as material: brushed, glass, chrome, holo. Read up close and quiet at a distance.

6

Generative engines

Reaction-diffusion, flow fields and boids run underneath and make content.

Lc 75+

Text contrast for numbers read in play (APCA), 60 for labels, 30 for anything disabled.

≤ 3 / s

Flashes over 2.78% of the screen (WCAG 2.3.1). It caught our own parallax window flickering, and soft edges with bilinear sampling fixed it.

600 ms

Minimum wind-up for a familiar attack: notice, decide, act, plus network latency. New attacks get 1 s or more.

500 ms

Gap between reward beats, so the second one doesn't vanish in the attentional blink.

ΔE 0.04

Colour-blind safe ladders. It caught Rare and Epic collapsing under deuteranopia, and Rare moved to a lighter blue.
