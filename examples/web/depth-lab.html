Depth without shaders

Roblox has no custom shaders, but an EditableImage can be redrawn every frame, and stacked geometry gets real parallax from the engine. Each plate is one technique from the Luau library, running here in JS. Move a pointer over a plate to move the eye. The orange number is this browser's frame cost; the grey one is the measured headless Luau cost for the same size.

Parallax occlusion window

256² · 16 steps– ms JS · 18 ms Luau

The surface is flat. Each texel marches a ray into a height field until it hits, so cobbles occlude each other and the cracks look deep. Use it for portals, grates, gem interiors and lava pits on a 128–256² image.

Interior mapping

256² · 4×4 rooms– ms JS · 11 ms Luau

One ray-box hit per texel puts a whole room behind every window: back wall, side walls, checker floor and a lamp. Lights are on or off at random. A city facade costs one image, not thousands of parts.

Infinite zoom

256² · 6 octaves– ms JS · 6–8 ms Luau

Six copies of a tile at scales 2k − frac(z), faded in at the small end and out at the large end. When one octave reaches the next, the image is identical, so it never pops. Spin turns it into a spiral. Any algorithm render works as the tile.

Shell fur

16 shells · alpha-tested– ms JS (software render)

Copies of the mesh pushed out along the normals, each with a sparser mask of strand dots that taper to a point. Strands get a real silhouette and real depth from the engine. In Roblox it's one MeshPart per shell, so the cost is draw calls, not Luau.

Layered 2D parallax

512×224 · 5 layers– ms JS · 3 ms Luau (256×144, 4 layers)

Each layer moves by the camera offset times its nearness. In Roblox you'd normally stack ImageLabels and move them, which costs nothing. The CPU composite exists for layers that are themselves procedural.
