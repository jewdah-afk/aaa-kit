# Research calculator

A single-page calculator for incremental-game research levels: total multiplier, research per second, cost of the next level, time per level, time from level A to level B, and a boosts and formulas reference. Your inputs save in the browser.

Open `index.html` in a browser. There's no build step and nothing is sent anywhere.

It moved here from the research-calculator repo, which is now a prototype bench (see [REPO-MAP](../../docs/REPO-MAP.md)).
